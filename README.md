**Global health, projects and research.**

I connect grant management with on-the-ground project delivery, drawing on more than 10 years of experience in public health programmes, international development and community grant management.

## Portfolio highlights

- **Programme delivery:** Coordinating partners, workplans, budgets and implementation across international health programmes.
- **Grants and governance:** Managing a portfolio of 146 funded projects, with experience monitoring and evaluating more than 500 initiatives.
- **Evidence and learning:** Applying monitoring and evaluation, data analysis and evidence synthesis to support decisions.

## Selected work

- **Lao PDR:** Chronic kidney disease care capacity building with medical professionals and local partner institutions.
- **Burkina Faso:** KOICA-funded infectious disease prevention and response in primary care, with responsibility for financial closeout, reporting, field monitoring and donor compliance checks from February to July 2023.
- **Mongolia:** Physician CPD (2014–2017) and medical university faculty development (2015–2017), as Project Coordinator at Raphael International.
- **Seoul:** Community development funding, from proposal review and grant setup to monitoring and closeout.
- **Research:** An MSc umbrella review of municipal solid waste management and human health, focusing on exposure types and the representation of low- and middle-income countries.
